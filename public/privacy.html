Democracy FC Privacy Policy

Democracy FC (the website at democracy-fc.netlify.app) does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Everything you do in the game — your universes, votes, predictions and coins — is saved only on your own device, in your browser's storage. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Coins are earned in the game and can never be bought.

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the standard request information any website does.

Democracy FC is not directed at children under 13.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/NabilPervez/democracy-fc.
